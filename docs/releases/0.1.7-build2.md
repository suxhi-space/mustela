### Security
- **Sign-in in the system keychain.** Windows Credential Manager, macOS Keychain, the Linux keyring, and encrypted storage on Android. It moves over by itself, no need to sign in again.
- Other security tightening around links, media, embeds and the sync server.

### Fixed
- **Saved themes disappearing on every device** when another device updated from an older version.
- Small fixes and polish: swipe between tabs and swipe back in Settings on phones, Mustela's title bar on the call pop-out (Windows), and phones staying upright outside videos and streams.

### Good to know
- **macOS:** after updating, your Mac may ask if Mustela can use its keychain. Click **Always Allow** (it may ask again after future updates until Mustela is signed by Apple).
- **Linux:** if no keyring (GNOME Keyring or KWallet) is running, your sign-in stays in Mustela's app data **unencrypted**, same as 0.1.7-build1. Start one and Mustela moves it over next time it opens.
- **Locked keychain:** if it's locked when Mustela starts, Mustela tells you instead of signing you out. Unlock it and restart Mustela.
- **Sync servers** see your sign-in once when you link them, so only link ones you trust.
