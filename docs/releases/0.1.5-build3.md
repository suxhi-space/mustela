## Mustela 0.1.5-build3

Fixes on top of 0.1.5-build2. This is source only. Installers still have to be built from this folder.

- macOS 26 glass icon: the Mac build uses the Icon Composer file as-is and the name `actool` writes, so the flat icon is no longer what macOS 26 shows. Needs Xcode 26 on the Mac that builds it. The disk image uses that same icon, because the image was still carrying the flat one.
- Bottom sheets stay as tall as their content and ease up instead of snapping to a full empty sheet.
- Phone profile cards follow text size, density, and readable text.
- An Instagram reel and a mirror link of the same reel show as one card.
- Videos, including embeds, can go full screen. Search’s Media tab includes those embeds.
- Picture-in-picture and the mini call can shrink to about a phone’s size. The mini call is flat.
- Android’s launcher icon is the full rounded tile, not a circle crop.
- Tray icon is the normal slot size on Windows, Linux, and Mac.
- Android reapplies the screen insets when you switch between gesture navigation and the 3-button bar.
- Avatar decorations in messages, the member list, and non-video call tiles are no longer cut off at the row.
- Screen share from Mustela’s picker stays at 30 frames a second for 720p and 1080p, and 60 for the smoother option. Stoat’s app and Discord send the screen straight to the system video encoder; Mustela still takes its own pictures first, so a share costs more than those apps. System sound still leaves Mustela out, so people in the call are not sent back to themselves.
- On a phone, tapping a stream you’re watching hides Mustela’s bars immediately. Full screen stays inside Mustela. The phone’s status bar hides with those bars and comes back when they do. A full-screen button sits on the stream.
- A short profile card stays as tall as its contents. A picture, video, or GIF that fails to load leaves no empty block. YouTube cards are unchanged.
