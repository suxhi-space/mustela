### New
- **Screen share sound on Mac and Linux.** The share picker offers the same choice as Windows: **This app** (the window you share), **Everything but Mustela**, or **No sound**. Mustela's own sound (the call) is always left out, so nobody hears themselves back.
  - Linux uses PipeWire: the app's sound streams (including ones from its helper processes, like a browser's) are wired straight into the share, and new ones (a new tab, the next song) are picked up as they start. Microphones and other devices are never included.
  - Mac uses Apple's ScreenCaptureKit (macOS 13 and newer).
  - If the sound can't start, the picture still goes out and a short message says why.
- **Invites with limits** (Stoat 0.16): server menu > Invite with limits, with "Expire after" (30 minutes to never) and "Max uses". The invite list in Server settings shows how many times each was used and when it expires.
- **.rpm** for Fedora and openSUSE, next to the AppImage and .deb. Updates for it work like the .deb: Mustela downloads it and opens your system's installer.

### Improved
- **Call pop-out on Windows** is a real Mustela window, like on Mac and Linux: Mustela's title bar (following the Title bar setting), "Mustela call" as its title, Mustela's icon in the taskbar, and no address bar. Settings > Voice & Video > Call pop-out can switch to Windows' own pop-up.
- **Easier to read colours.**
  - Name colours that almost disappear on the background (a very light pink on light mode, a very dark one on dark mode) are shown in a readable shade of the same colour. The colour people picked never changes, only how it's shown. Role colours too.
  - Glow and Neon name styles stay readable in light mode.
  - Names on a nameplate get a soft cushion behind the letters; offline people fade less in light mode.
  - Profile cards and full profiles: buttons ("Message", "Edit profile") use that person's profile colours instead of your theme, and headings and the activity box are checked against the card's background.
- **Updates on computers** don't show a card anymore (they install when Mustela starts). An update that's waiting (a .deb or .rpm, or one found with Check in Settings) shows as a small green download button in the title bar, or at the bottom of the server list with the system's title bar.
- **Phones:** the update card only has Update and Later.
- **Deleting a server you own or a bot** asks for your password or 2FA code first, as Stoat 0.17 requires.
- **Emoji from other servers** are greyed out in the picker and for reactions in servers that don't allow them ("Use external emojis", Stoat 0.16), instead of failing to send.
- Stoat's JavaScript library updated to its newest version.
- Release files can now be signed with Mustela's own update key, so installed copies only install updates signed by it (on top of GitHub's checksum).

### Fixed
- **Mac:** messages, pictures and avatars drawn over the "You're viewing older messages" bar.
- The updater offered .deb installs to .rpm and Flatpak installs.

### Good to know
- **Mac:** sharing sound needs macOS 13 or newer. It uses the Screen Recording permission you already gave Mustela for the picture (macOS calls it "Screen & System Audio Recording"). Older Macs still run Mustela, just without the sound.
- **Linux:** sharing sound needs PipeWire, the default on most distros now (Fedora, Ubuntu 22.10+, Mint 22, PikaOS and others). On Wayland, some windows don't say which app they belong to; for those, the picker only offers everything but Mustela.
- **Windows:** if the call pop-out ever freezes, close Mustela and open it again: it switches to Windows' own pop-up and tells you. If that happens, `popout.log` in Mustela's log folder shows where it stopped.
- **Sharing everything but Mustela** includes notification sounds and anything else playing on your computer.
