## Version: 0.1.7-build1 · Polish & Themes

**A big polish update.** Mustela opens straight away, Home finally has a proper search, phones feel smoother to use, and you can now save your whole look as a theme. Plus a lot of fixes across mobile and desktop.

### New
- **Saved themes.** Save your whole look under a name and switch back to it any time: colours, gradient, background, panel see-through and blur, depth, text size and window material. Themes follow your account to every device. A background picture stays on the device you picked it on; on your other devices Mustela asks you to pick it once.
- **Search on Home.** On phones, the search button opens a search for people (friends and server members), messages in your DMs, media, pins, links, files and channels, with filters (from, in, has, mentions, dates). On desktop, "Find or start a conversation" (or Ctrl+K) jumps to anyone or anywhere.
- **Double tap a message to react** (phones and tablets), with the emoji of your choice.
- **Your photos in the + card** (Android). Pick photos right from the card, see them all or by date, and drag the scroll handle to jump through the months.

### Improved
- **Fast start-up.** Opens straight away from where you left off and connects in the background.
- **Android notifications** work like a chat app's: one per chat, Reply and Mark as read from the notification, cleared when you read it anywhere.
- **Smoother gestures on phones.** Hold cards open halfway and all work the same way, cards drag from anywhere, swiping back feels smoother.
- **Emoji and GIF card.** Slides between Emoji and GIFs, a delete key, GIFs load faster.
- **Search everywhere.** Results look like messages, and the Media tab scrolls properly.
- **Settings.** A settings search that finds every setting, categories in Server settings, server and channel settings one page at a time on phones, and a crop tool for server icons and banners.
- **Big servers run lighter.** A new Performance option (on by default) holds profile effects still in the chat and member list of servers with over 1,000 members. Profile cards still play when you open them.
- **Voice.** Mic and headphone menus with volume sliders, and input sensitivity that shows your live mic level.
- **UI updates on mobile and desktop.** Round send button, bigger status dots, cleaner server badges, long names end in "…", and a proper "Delete message?" pop-up.

### Fixed
- Gradient themes showing blocks and seams
- Save, Share and Open in browser not working in the picture viewer on phones
- The keyboard hiding dialogs and cards on phones and tablets
- Search pictures overlapping, and the message box showing over search
- Switching servers sometimes needing two clicks
- GIFs and animated emoji still playing with the setting off
- The emoji list not jumping to sections above where you are
- Link previews showing only one picture (X, Tumblr)
- Plus lots of smaller fixes
