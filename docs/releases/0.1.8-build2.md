### New
- **Mustela servers** (was "Profile servers"). Mustela's own server is in your list already, so you see other people's decorations, banners and activity. Press **Link** to share yours.
- **Several activities at once**, like Discord: a game and music together, on your card and for friends.
- **Your own games and apps** (Settings > Activity > Add a program): pick something running, like Krita, and Mustela shows it.
- **Screen share sound on Mac and Linux.** Share just that app's sound, or everything but Mustela.
- **Other people's sounds** volume (Voice & Video): turn down everyone's soundboard, just for you.
- **View a server as a role** (Server settings > Roles, the eye button).
- **Windows and Linux on ARM.**
- **Invites with limits** (expire after, max uses), and **.rpm** for Fedora and openSUSE.

### Improved
- **Activity reaches your friends and your other devices** reliably, and stays up to date on screen.
- **Your own card** shows when you're in voice, and your computer's activity on your phone.
- **Rich Presence to Discord** works when Discord opens after the game.
- **Phones:** swipe the call down to minimise it. "Streams in PiP" option on Android.
- **Easier to read colours:** names keep their own colour in light mode with no grey behind them, and profile cards use that person's colours.
- **Computers** install updates at start-up; a waiting one shows as a small download button in the title bar.
- Call messages say how long the call lasted. Deleting a server or bot asks for your password, as Stoat now requires.
- Faster and lighter: Mustela servers get one request for many profiles, and unused code was cleaned out.

### Fixed
- **Mac:** messages and pictures drawn over the "viewing older messages" bar.
- If a Mustela server forgets your device, Mustela links it again by itself.
- Small fixes: emoji from other servers greyed out where a server doesn't allow them, no "Skip this version" on phones.

### Good to know
- **Mac:** sharing sound needs macOS 13 or newer. **Linux:** it needs PipeWire (the default on most distros).
- **Windows:** the call pop-out is Windows' own pop-up by default. Settings > Voice & Video > Call pop-out has "Mustela's window (experimental)" to try.
- **Host your own Mustela server:** see `docs/hosting.md` (about $5 a month).
