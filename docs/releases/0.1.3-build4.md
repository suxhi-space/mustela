## Mustela 0.1.3-build4

First public build. Mustela is a client for [Stoat](https://stoat.chat) that adds calls, screen share, profile decorations and a lot of small things on top. Sign in with your normal Stoat account and everything stays in sync with Stoat's own apps.

It's a beta, so things will break here and there. If something does, please open an issue and say what you were doing and which system you're on.

### Downloads

| System | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10/11 | `Mustela_0.1.3-build4_windows_x64.exe` |
| Linux | `Mustela_0.1.3-build4_linux_amd64.AppImage` |
| Android | `Mustela_0.1.3-build4_android_universal.apk` |

Not code-signed yet. On Windows, if SmartScreen shows up, click **More info** then **Run anyway**.

### Calls
- Voice in voice channels, DMs and groups, with mute, deafen and a full call view
- Camera and screen share. Pick a whole screen or a single app window from Mustela's own picker
- Stream sound: share one app's audio, or everything except Mustela. Plays back in stereo
- On Windows, screen share uses one continuous capture, so no flickering mouse pointer and windows behind others still work
- Watch or stop watching someone's stream, click any tile to make it big, full screen
- Right-click someone in a call: their volume (up to 200%), mute them just for you, their stream's volume
- Hide people without video
- Input sensitivity (automatic or manual with a meter), echo cancellation, noise suppression, device choice
- Soundboard: add clips from a file or a link, trim to 10 seconds, play them into the call. Works while you're muted, and you can set a join sound
- Pop the call out into its own window and back again without reconnecting
- In a call and reading another chat? The stream sits in a resizable preview above the member list
- The call bar shows which channel and server you're in
- If a call drops, Mustela tries to rejoin once by itself
- Linux systems without WebRTC in WebKitGTK can still do voice (it runs natively in Rust)
- Screen share qualities match what Stoat allows: 720p, and 1080p if your account has it

### Android
- Calls keep going when you swipe the app away, and use call volume instead of media volume
- A floating call bubble over other apps: tap to come back, hold to mute
- Screen sharing from your phone
- On phones the call fills the screen, with the chat one tap away, and a small draggable tile follows you around the app
- The back button goes back a screen instead of closing the app
- Padding around the status bar, nav bar and keyboard
- Android 12+ can use your wallpaper colours

### Profiles
Seen by other Mustela users. Stoat's apps see your normal profile.
- Avatar decorations, profile effects, card frames and nameplates
- Name fonts, name effects and profile colours
- Profile editor with a live preview of your card, how you look in chat, and your nameplate
- A different profile per server: nickname, avatar, banner, about me, decorations and colours
- Cropper for avatars and banners
- Choose who sees your Mustela profile: everyone, friends, or friends plus servers you pick
- Each decoration has a performance rating, and you set the heaviest one you're willing to see moving
- Roles and "member since" on someone's card inside a server, and you can add or remove roles if you're allowed
- Profiles refresh when you open them
- Game activity: what you're playing is picked up automatically, plus Rich Presence from games that support Discord's

### Chat
- Drafts saved per channel and DM, even after closing the app
- Mentions, channels and roles show as chips while typing
- Formatting bar that follows your selection, plus Ctrl/Cmd+B, I and U
- Spoiler option on uploads
- Autocomplete for channels, roles, `@everyone` and `@online`
- "N new messages since…" with Jump and Mark as read, and a New line. DMs open at your first unread message
- Inbox for mentions, click one to jump straight to it
- New DMs show above your server list with a badge
- Read-only channels say so, slowmode shows a countdown
- Messages you missed while reconnecting load by themselves
- Notifications respect Busy and Focus, and skip blocked people
- Emoji in any of Stoat's packs, used everywhere including reactions and statuses
- Friends page shows who's in voice, who's streaming and what they're playing

### Servers and channels
- Favorite channels from any server, listed on Home
- Pin channels to the top of a server
- Pin DMs into named, coloured groups
- Drag to rearrange channels and categories (if you can manage them, saved to Stoat)
- Server folders
- Going back to a server opens the channel you were last in
- Right-click menus for channels, categories and empty space
- Channel settings: overview, permissions per role, webhooks
- Server settings: system message channels, Discover, delete server
- Discover works, with invites and bots opening inside Mustela

### Look
- 3D style with floating panels and chunky buttons, or flat
- 9 colour themes or any colour, palette styles, contrast levels, light, dark and pure black
- Gradient accent
- Backgrounds: stars, plain, or your own picture
- Mica and Acrylic on Windows, vibrancy on macOS, a slim title bar (or the system one)
- Message size, spacing, font and density

### Desktop
- Tray icon with mute, deafen and leave
- Start with your computer, close to tray
- Updates itself from this page (Windows and the AppImage install in place)
- The AppImage can install itself into your app menu with an icon
- Several accounts with quick switching

### Performance
- Animations pause when Mustela isn't in focus
- Lighter animations during calls and while streaming
- GIFs and animated emoji can be turned off separately from decorations
- Effects made much lighter, and they can't freeze the app anymore
- Blur on or off

### Sync server
Optional and self-hostable. It shares profiles and activity between Mustela users. Version 1.2 adds per-server banners. It never stores your Stoat session.
