## Mustela 0.1.5-build1: the big mobile update

Mobile has been the rough side of Mustela since the start. This update rebuilds how it works on phones and tablets: they now have their own set of screens made for touch (swipes, cards from the bottom, the phone's own edges respected), separate from desktop, so fixing one can't break the other anymore. It still looks like Mustela and every feature is still there.

Desktop gets a few things too: faster big servers, role order and icons, and soundboard improvements.

### Phones and tablets
- Phones and tablets have their own screens now, separate from the desktop ones, so fixing one no longer breaks the other. Each download only carries its own
- Tablets held upright use the phone layout; held sideways they keep the desktop layout, now clear of the status bar and nav buttons
- Gesture navigation no longer leaves a big empty strip at the bottom (tablets especially). Nav buttons and a pinned taskbar still get their room
- Swipe right in a chat to slide back to the channel list, swipe left for the members list, and swipe left on the channel list to go back into your chat
- Profile cards come up from the bottom, the width of the screen, with everything on them: mutual servers and friends included. Pull a card down to close it. On a tablet held sideways the card is centred
- Hold menus close with a pull down too
- The "viewing older messages" bar is the slim one from desktop, attached to the message box (no more big Jump to present block), and "N new messages" fits on one line
- Settings scroll again on phones
- Home is "Messages" on phones, like Discord's app: find a conversation, Friends and a new message button up top, then "Active now" scrolling sideways (a big card for each voice channel your friends are in or for what they're playing, a small square for friends who are just online), then your conversations with "In voice" or "Streaming" under the name. Discover is gone from phones (Stoat doesn't let other apps show it inside them anyway)
- The new message button: pick one friend to message them, or a few to start a group
- Before you join a voice channel you see everyone's pictures like on desktop, with Chat and Join along the bottom
- Streams in a call stay in the grid until you tap one to make it bigger (watching one doesn't take over the screen anymore). Only then do the controls step aside, and only a stream you've made bigger turns sideways like a video
- Each stream you're watching has a button to stop watching and one to make it bigger or smaller
- Your own stream shows "You're sharing your screen!" with a Stop sharing button instead of a copy of your screen, which saves your phone a lot of work
- The mini call view has no mute button anymore: it's square with whoever's talking, or wide with the stream you're watching, and you can pinch it bigger or smaller
- Picture in picture shows the same thing as the mini view, filling the little window. Tapping it opens the call; opening Mustela any other way takes you back to where you were
- Your own stream never shows in the mini view or picture in picture
- Your own card (tap your name at the bottom) comes up from the bottom like everyone else's, and Edit profile opens your main profile on phones
- The "Voice connected" bar is bigger with bigger buttons, and sits right on top of your profile bar
- On phones that bar has a mute button instead of screen share: tap to mute or unmute, hold to deafen (it shows when you're deafened), tap again to undeafen
- Smoother: screens slide in, sheets and menus ease up, buttons give a little when you press them (all off with Reduce motion)
- Easier to tap everywhere: a taller message box whose hint stays on one line, chat headers that leave room for the name, a full-width search box in chats, bigger rows in settings, channels and members, and bigger emoji
- The sound button at the top of a call shows a speaker with sound coming out
- In a call, tapping the channel name at the top opens the channel's menu (the chat has its own button)
- Calls on Android: voices, streams and soundboard sounds should all play at call volume now, like a phone call (in build4 only the soundboard preview did; everything in the call was still on media volume). Per-person volume goes up to 200% again

- Moving a call to your phone (or any device) never replays your entry sound, wherever you joined from

### Servers
- Big servers no longer freeze things while the member list loads, and switching channels doesn't wait for it: it loads 25 people at a time as you scroll, after the channel's messages
- What people's banners say (their nameplates and decorations) is kept on your device for a day, so lists don't download everyone's banner again. Opening someone's card always gets the latest
- Change the order of roles by dragging them in Server settings > Roles
- Roles can have an icon, and like Stoat it shows next to people's names in chat and the member list (their highest role that has one)
- Channels you can't send messages in no longer show a see-through message box

### App size
- New in Accessibility on phones and tablets: App size makes the whole app bigger or smaller together (text, buttons, icons and spacing), from 80% to 175%. By default it follows your phone's own font size setting, which Mustela ignored before

### Text size
- The text size slider (Appearance and Accessibility) now also makes the rest of the app's text a little bigger or smaller (channels, members, settings, menus, headers), not just messages

### Syncing
- Soundboard sounds you added on your other devices show up as soon as Mustela opens, not only after you open the soundboard
- Coming back to Mustela after a while picks up settings and favorites you changed on your other devices meanwhile

### Soundboard
- Add sounds from Soundboard settings, from a file or a link, not just from a call
- Drag sounds to a new place on the board
