## Mustela 0.1.4-build3

A big round of fixes and requests from testing: phones got a lot of attention, calls got smarter about moving between devices, and there's a new emoji picker, colour picker and Accessibility page.

### Downloads

| System | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10/11 | `Mustela_0.1.4-build3_windows_x64.exe` |
| macOS | `Mustela_0.1.4-build3_macos_universal.dmg` |
| Linux | `Mustela_0.1.4-build3_linux_amd64.AppImage` |
| Android | `Mustela_0.1.4-build3_android_universal.apk` |

Desktop now updates itself when it starts, so after this one you shouldn't need to come back here.

### Updates
- Desktop checks for an update once when Mustela starts and installs it right away, like Discord. No more "a new version is out" card on Windows, macOS or the AppImage
- macOS installs updates in place: no more dragging the app into Applications
- Turn it off in **Settings > Advanced** if you'd rather update yourself

### Calls
- Joining from another device moves the call over cleanly. The device you left shows "Your voice has been disconnected because you connected at another location" with a Reconnect button (desktop)
- If Mustela was closed while you were in a call, it offers to put you back in when you open it (desktop)
- Phones: a cleaner call screen. A simple bar on top (minimise, the channel, sound, chat), big tiles with names centred at the bottom, and camera, mic, screen share, soundboard and hang up along the bottom
- Phones: the controls and top bar hide after a few seconds so the call or stream gets the whole screen. Tap to bring them back
- Phones: tap a stream and it fills the screen, with the phone's status bar and nav buttons out of the way too. Turn your phone sideways and it plays like a video, even with rotation locked
- Phones: the rest of the time the status bar and nav buttons stay put and nothing hides under them
- Phones: the speaker button at the top picks where the sound plays (phone, speaker, Bluetooth, headphones), the call volume, and deafen
- Hold on someone in a call for their options
- Picture in picture shows only the call: square while you're just talking, wide while you watch a stream
- Option to hide the little call tile that follows you around the app (the eye button on the call card)
- The Web (video) engine keeps calls going in the background on Android too, with a notification to come back or leave
- Soundboard has a backup way to play into the call on phones that can't mix it into the microphone
- Your entry sound follows you to every device
- Clips added from a link already did; clips added from a file still stay on that device

### Phones and tablets
- Every hold menu is a card that slides up from the bottom, with big rows
- Bigger, easier-to-read text on phones and tablets (Accessibility can turn it off)
- Holding on a channel, button or category no longer selects the text around it
- Settings: solid behind the gesture bar, scroll all the way, and section headings stay on top
- Tap the name or picture at the top of a DM for their card (the extra button is gone)
- "Jump to present" is a bar above the message box
- Editing a message happens in the message box at the bottom, marked "Editing message"
- The formatting bar sits under the message box on phones, so the phone's own copy and paste menu no longer covers it
- Favorites is a button in Home on phones, or under the Home button if you prefer
- Stoat invite links opened from other apps can open in Mustela

### Chat
- Link previews like Discord's for social posts: X, Instagram (reels too), Bluesky, TikTok and Reddit show the picture or video right in the chat, so you don't have to open them
- YouTube videos play right in the chat
- Videos in other link previews play inline too
- "You're viewing older messages" floats just above the message box on desktop
- Message search works on phones, with filters and tabs (from the last build) and now faster
- Mark as unread marks the right message, and it stays unread until you leave the chat
- DMs show how many messages are unread, not just a dot
- The "new messages" bar sits above the messages instead of covering them
- Editing a message shows mentions and channels as names, not their long IDs
- View reactions: see who reacted with what (in the message menu, or right-click a reaction)
- Names in messages and voice lists keep their font but drop the colour effects, so chat stays readable
- The GIF star only shows when you hover a GIF on desktop; on phones it's in the hold menu
- Discover opens in its own window on desktop (Stoat doesn't allow it inside other apps), and in your browser on phones

### Emoji
- New emoji picker: one list you scroll through, with Favorites, Frequently used, then your servers in the same order as your server list
- Favorite any emoji with the star, right-click, or hold. Favorites sync between devices
- The emoji button in your status works

### Look
- Mustela's own colour picker with an Apply button, used everywhere colours are picked
- "Advanced color picker" under each decoration's colour
- Panel see-through actually works now, and goes further
- "Blur behind panels" switch
- Colours from your background picture (the picture swatch next to the system one)
- Turn off colour sync for one device in **Appearance**, so it keeps its own theme (your profile, favorites and the rest still sync)
- Tray icons have light and dark versions that follow your system

### Accessibility
- A new **Accessibility** page: text size and spacing, easier-to-read messages, bigger text on touch screens, underlined links, names in role colours on or off, contrast, density and reduce motion

### Profiles and servers
- Rich Presence looks like Discord's: "Listening to YouTube Music", the song, artist and album, a progress bar and buttons
- Rich Presence shows the app's real name instead of "Something"
- Member since shows both when they joined Stoat and when they joined the server, with its icon
- The status dot sits on top of the profile picture again
- Profile animations no longer freeze when you open a card in a busy chat
- New role button sits next to the Roles heading
- Changing a role's colour no longer errors

### Favorites
- The Favorites heading always shows on Home, so you can always right-click it
- Dragging favorites no longer opens a big gap in the list
- Categories can have no colour

### Desktop
- Choose whether Mustela opens its window or stays in the tray when it starts with your computer (**Settings > Desktop**)
- Notifications show the sender's picture and where it came from, and clicking one opens the chat (Windows)

### Safety
- Avatars used in notifications are decoded with size limits
- Mustela's pages can't be framed by other sites or submit forms to them
- Updates are still only taken from your own GitHub releases and checked against GitHub's SHA-256 before they install
