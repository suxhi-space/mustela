## Mustela 0.1.5-build2: a new look

Mustela has a new icon. It's the same little face everywhere: on Windows, Linux and Android, on the Mac (as a real Liquid Glass icon on macOS 26 and later), in Android's notifications, and in the tray, where its dot now shows your status at a glance.

There's plenty more besides: tablets get a proper call screen, pictures open in a new viewer, search has Discord's filters, and a long list of fixes for phones and tablets.

### The new icon
- New app icon on Windows, macOS, Linux and Android
- On macOS 26 and later it's a layered Liquid Glass icon, made in Apple's Icon Composer (when the Mac that built it has Xcode 26). Older macOS gets a flat version of the same design
- The tray icon's dot follows your status: green online, yellow idle, blue focus, red busy, grey invisible. It gets a white centre while you're in a call
- Android's notification icon is the new face too
- The sign-in screen and the title bar use it as well

### Fixes
- Chats you'd already read sometimes reopened partway up at an old message. A message that arrived while you were reading wasn't being marked read, so the chat remembered the wrong spot. It's marked read properly now, including when you leave the chat, and on Android
- Instagram reels: Mustela now asks more of the sites that fix Instagram links, all at once, and tries a few different bot identities, so reels get a playable video more often. This works for both instagram.com and kkinstagram.com links
- Joining a voice channel on Android no longer crashes the app if the microphone permission hasn't been given yet
- Links aren't underlined anymore when "Underline links" is off (they still underline on hover)
- Menus, the emoji and GIF pickers, the soundboard and other pop-ups have a solid background now instead of being see-through
- Tapping an emoji in someone's status no longer opens the emoji info card

### Phones and tablets
- Tablets held upright get the two-pane layout (channel list next to the chat), like Discord. Phones keep the one-screen layout
- Discover is gone from tablets too
- The Messages header (search, Friends, new message) stays put while you scroll your conversations
- "Active now" isn't cut off at the bottom anymore
- Conversations in the list are closer together
- Settings work like their own screen: a back / close button at the top of the main page, and each section opens as its own page. Opening Voice & Video from a call menu, or soundboard settings from the soundboard, takes you straight to that page instead of the main settings list
- The member list in a server comes up as a card from the bottom, like profile cards
- Cards from the bottom (profiles, member list, your own card, menus) slide up to the top of the screen when you scroll or pull them up, stopping under the status bar. Pull down to close as before
- The soundboard is a proper bottom sheet with bigger tiles, and its settings page is laid out for a phone screen
- Picture-in-picture: you can resize it like other apps' PiP, the picture inside is sized right, and coming back to the app no longer leaves it zoomed with scroll bars

### Pictures and videos
- New picture viewer on phones and tablets. It's full screen and black like a phone gallery: swipe sideways to go through pictures, pinch or double-tap to zoom, tap to hide the bars, and pull down to close
- On computers it keeps the old look: the picture straight on the dimmed app, no box, about the size of the full profile. Click around it to close. It has arrows, double-click or scroll-wheel zoom, the arrow keys, and a bar under the picture with who sent it, the file name, View original and the ⋮ menu
- On computers, a picture from search or chat only steps through the pictures sent with it in the same message. Phones swipe through all of search's pictures like a gallery
- Several pictures in one message are grouped into a grid like Discord's (two side by side, one big and two stacked, rows of three…). Click one to swipe through them all
- Who sent it and when is at the bottom, with a strip of the other pictures to jump between
- The ⋮ menu has Save, Share, Open in browser and Show in chat. On Android, Save goes to Downloads and Share opens the phone's share menu
- Search's Media tab is a grid now, three across on phones, with who sent each one in the corner. Tapping one opens the viewer, and you can swipe through all of them from there. "Show in chat" takes you to the message
- A message with several pictures lets you swipe between them
- Spoiler pictures stay blurred in search and in the viewer's strip until you tap them

### Search
- Search has Discord's filters: from someone, has (media, link, file), mentions someone, sent on / before / after a day, and people or bots. They work without any search words too, e.g. every message from someone last week

### Calls
- Tiles are laid out like Discord: all the same size, as big as fits. In a crowded call they stay readable and you scroll
- Tablets held upright use the phone's call screen
- Bigger tablets held sideways keep the desktop look (the chat beside the call), with bigger buttons, and a full-screen button that switches to the phone's call screen across the whole tablet. Exit full screen or tap the chat button to go back
- Small tablets (about 7 inches) use the phone's call screen sideways too
- On tablets, leaving a call's screen shows the mini view (like phones) instead of the desktop call preview
- Call controls hide on their own again after a few seconds, whether or not you're looking at a stream
- On phones, opening a stream shows only that stream. The people and other streams come back when you close it

### Home and favorites
- Favorites in Home is a single button like Saved Notes. Tap it to unfold your favorite channels and groups underneath, tap again to fold them away. Same on desktop and mobile
- The conversation you have open shows that person's nameplate behind it

### Settings
- New "Sync colors with" option in Appearance: all your devices, only your phones and tablets (or only your computers), or just this device. Your profile, favorites and everything else still sync everywhere
