## Mustela 0.1.4-build4

Streams that don't blast into your ears, a soundboard redo, mature channels, better link previews, every emoji, and a fix for app screen share sometimes not starting on Windows.

### Downloads

| System | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10/11 | `Mustela_0.1.4-build4_windows_x64.exe` |
| macOS | `Mustela_0.1.4-build4_macos_universal.dmg` |
| Linux | `Mustela_0.1.4-build4_linux_amd64.AppImage` |
| Android | `Mustela_0.1.4-build4_android_universal.apk` |

If you're on build3 on desktop, this one installs itself when you open Mustela.

### Streams
- Nobody's stream starts playing by itself any more. You press Watch when you want it
- When a stream you're watching ends, its spot says "This stream has ended" with a Close stream button. Leave it open and if they go live again it picks back up there; close it and you'll press Watch next time. Same on phones
- While you're sharing, the share button opens Stop streaming and Change stream (pick something else to share without stopping first)
- Click someone in a voice channel's list in the sidebar for their profile card, beside the list so it doesn't cover the names
- "Hide people without video" also hides streams you aren't watching, and it's greyed out when nobody has a camera or stream on

### Moving calls between devices
- Moving a call to another device no longer plays your entry sound again, and neither does Mustela rejoining by itself after a drop

### Android calls
- One call engine: the web engine (camera, screen share, watching streams) is now the only one, so the Call engine choice is gone
- Locking your phone or leaving the app no longer leaves you "in the call" but actually disconnected: the call keeps the phone awake enough to stay connected, the page keeps running in the background, and if the connection still dies Mustela notices and rejoins (also when you come back to the app)
- Call sound (voices, streams and soundboard) plays on the phone's call volume, like a phone call, not media volume. Per-person volume goes up to 100% on Android
- The "In voice call" notification with Leave stays while you're in a call

### Soundboard
- New layout: a search bar, your favorites on top, then all your sounds in a grid
- Point at a sound for a preview button (only you hear it) and a star to favorite it. On phones both are always there
- Favorite sounds sync between your devices
- A Soundboard settings page (from **Voice & video**, or the gear in the soundboard): your entry sound, and each sound's emoji (server emoji work too), name and volume. Sounds added from a link keep those changes on every device
- Adding a sound in a call uses the real emoji picker, server emoji included

### Calls
- A sound when someone starts a stream, and another when it ends (turn them off in **Settings > Notifications**)
- A clearer sound when someone leaves the call, so it doesn't sound like someone joining
- Testing your mic in **Voice & video** while you're in a call mutes and deafens you until you stop, so you hear only yourself and nobody hears the test
- Windows: sharing an app sometimes showed "No picture came from that window" until Mustela was restarted. Fixed, and if Windows is slow with the first picture Mustela now tries once more
- Windows: "Could not start audio source" no longer stops a share. If the sound can't start, the picture goes out without it. The microphone gets a second try too

### Chat
- Big servers open without the member list freezing things: it draws the first few dozen people and adds more as you scroll, and it remembers the list for a minute instead of downloading it every time
- Mature channels ask you to confirm you're 18 or older before showing them, like Stoat does (once per channel, on each device)
- Link previews for many more sites, plus the "fix the embed" links people paste instead (fxtwitter, vxtwitter, kkinstagram, ddinstagram, rxddit, fxbsky, xfuraffinity and the rest): X, Instagram, TikTok, Reddit, Bluesky, Fur Affinity, pixiv, DeviantArt, Tumblr and Threads
- Fur Affinity links show the picture
- Instagram reels and TikToks play right in the chat, even when Stoat only gave a still picture. If a video still won't play, the picture opens the post
- The emoji picker has every standard emoji now (🫂 and the rest that were missing), and the old short names like :joy: still work
- Scrolling up on desktop now shows the same "You're viewing older messages" bar with Jump to present, attached to the top of the message box and above everything in the chat (the old round button could end up behind pictures)
- The "new messages" bar no longer slides under the floating call preview, and its count matches the unread messages (it could say "37+" for 7)
- Emoji picked in a non-default emoji style no longer show a small box in front of them in the message box

### Android
- Fixed the Android build failing on newer build tools ("android:exported needs to be explicitly specified")
