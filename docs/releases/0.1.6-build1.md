## Version: 0.1.6-build1

**Android notifications now come through when Mustela is closed**, without showing you online or keeping your phone awake. And while you're in Mustela on your phone or tablet, new messages pop in as a little card at the top. Search works like Discord's now: filters as boxes that search straight away, the full number of results, and a tidy Media tab. Tablets held upright get the phone layout, cards on phones follow your finger, and there's a long list of fixes for phones, tablets and Discover. Plus friend nicknames, Copy image and Save image as.

### Added
- **Notifications when Mustela is closed (Android).** Mustela checks for new DMs and mentions about once a minute while you use your phone, and every 15 minutes or so while it's asleep. You stay offline, and your mutes and notification settings apply. Tap one to open the chat (Settings > Notifications to turn it off)
- New messages while you're in Mustela show as a card at the top (phones and tablets): tap it to open the chat, or swipe it away
- Friend nicknames: right-click a friend > Add friend nickname (only you see it, on all your devices)
- Copy image and Save image as… when you right-click a picture
- Search shows how many results there are and splits them into pages
- Search filters show as boxes in the search bar, and picking one searches straight away
- Drag yourself to another voice channel, or use Move to on yourself
- A sound when you move a call between your devices, so you know it worked
- Swipe up on a call (phones) to bring up all the controls
- Crop role icons when you upload them
- Android themed icons (when "Themed icons" is on)

### Changed
- Settings fills the window when it's about square (desktop); wide windows keep the card
- Tablets held upright use the phone layout
- The member list is a little wider and more compact (desktop)
- Clicking a search result jumps to it in the chat (desktop)
- Mustela goes offline 30 seconds after you leave it on your phone (unless you're in a call), like closing it
- Search's Media tab: no GIFs, link-preview pictures included, spoilers covered, video tiles show the video
- Clicking the name at the top of a DM opens their full profile
- Undeafening turns your mic back on, and mute and deafen stay as you left them for your next call
- Sessions are named after your device, like "Mustela for Windows (DESKTOP-4F2)"
- Pictures and videos in link previews stay a sensible size
- The emoji and GIF card follows your finger anywhere on it, and stays open to pick several emoji (phones)
- Every card from the bottom follows your finger; your own card can open full height (phones)
- Menus, pickers and pop-ups are always solid, whatever the panel opacity
- Phones: every screen's header is the same size
- Icons are a little bolder, with softer corners
- The + button opens a card with big Camera, Video, Photos, Files and Timestamp buttons (phones)
- App size: "Match my phone" is gone (it made the app come back from the background too big). 100% is the default

### Fixed
- Role icons not showing in server settings, with no way to remove them
- Discover: picking a server did nothing, and it could run past the edge of the window
- Search suggestions hidden behind pictures in the chat
- The app coming back from the background too big (phones)
- Picture-in-picture showing a huge picture and a cut-off name
- A blue box flashing over things you tap (Android)
- A grey strip behind the navigation bar (Android)
- The status bar staying when the picture viewer hides its buttons
- The call screen not reaching up behind the status bar
- Tablets held sideways: the message box jumping up and the user bar floating when the keyboard opens
- Emoji and GIFs not loading until Mustela was restarted
- Gaps beside the bars in Settings (phones)
- Clicking outside your card (from the bottom left) only closed it; now the click also does what you clicked (desktop)
- The emoji and GIF card jumping around, and a gap when going back to the keyboard (phones)
- Emoji and GIFs slow to show up, and the emoji picker slow to open (phones)
- Still signed in after uninstalling and reinstalling (Android)
