## Version: 0.1.6-build1

**Android notifications now come through when Mustela is closed**, without showing you online or keeping your phone awake. Search works more like Discord's, tablets held upright get the phone layout, and there's a long list of fixes, mostly for phones and tablets.

### Added
- **Notifications when Mustela is closed (Android).** New DMs and mentions within about a minute while you use your phone, or every 15 minutes or so while it's asleep. You stay offline (Settings > Notifications to turn it off)
- New messages show as a card at the top while you're in Mustela (phones and tablets)
- Friend nicknames: right-click a friend > Add friend nickname
- Copy image and Save image as… when you right-click a picture
- Search: filters as boxes that search straight away, the number of results, and pages

### Changed
- Tablets held upright use the phone layout
- Settings fills the window when it's about square (desktop)
- Pop-ups blur the app behind them (desktop)
- The + button opens a card with Camera, Video, Photos and Files (phones)
- Bolder icons
- App size: "Match my phone" is gone, 100% is the default

### Fixed
- Lag with the keyboard, emoji and GIFs, and the emoji and GIF card jumping around (phones)
- Emoji and GIFs slow to load, or not loading until Mustela was restarted
- The app coming back from the background too big (phones)
- Picture-in-picture showing a huge picture and a cut-off name
- Still signed in after uninstalling and reinstalling (Android)
- Undeafening not turning your mic back on
- Discover: picking a server did nothing
- Role icons not showing in server settings
- Search suggestions hidden behind pictures in the chat
- Tablets held sideways: the message box jumping when the keyboard opens
- Status bar, navigation bar and tap highlight glitches (Android)
