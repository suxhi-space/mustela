### New
- **Screen share sound on Mac and Linux.** Like on Windows: share just that app's sound, or everything but Mustela.
- **Invites with limits.** Pick when an invite expires and how many times it can be used.
- **.rpm for Fedora and openSUSE.**

### Improved
- **Call pop-out on Windows** is a real Mustela window now: Mustela's title bar and icon, no address bar.
- **Easier to read colours.** Very light or dark name colours get a readable shade (your colour itself doesn't change), and profile cards use that person's colours for their buttons and headings.
- **Updates on computers** don't show a card anymore. If one is waiting, a small download button appears in the title bar.
- Deleting a server or a bot asks for your password (or 2FA) first, as Stoat now requires.

### Fixed
- **Mac:** messages and pictures drawn over the "viewing older messages" bar.
- Small fixes and polish: emoji from other servers greyed out where a server doesn't allow them, and phones' update card has no "Skip this version".

### Good to know
- **Mac:** sharing sound needs macOS 13 or newer, and uses the Screen Recording permission you already gave Mustela.
- **Linux:** sharing sound needs PipeWire (the default on most distros now). On Wayland, some windows don't say which app they belong to; for those you can share everything but Mustela instead of just that app.
- **Windows:** if the call pop-out ever freezes, Mustela switches back to Windows' own pop-up the next time it opens and tells you. You can change it in Settings > Voice & Video.
