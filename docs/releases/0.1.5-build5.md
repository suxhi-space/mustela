## Version: 0.1.5-build5

Big servers no longer slow Mustela down or freeze it, your recent chats open instantly, and emoji show up straight away in your chosen style. Phones and tablets get a long list of fixes, and there are a few new things: scheduled messages, search filters on desktop, and moving people between voice channels.

### Added
- Scheduled messages: right-click Send (hold it on phones)
- Search filters on desktop (`from:`, `has:`, `mentions:`, `before:`, `after:`...) with suggestions, a Filters window and sorting
- Move people between voice channels, plus server mute and deafen (needs permission)
- Recent chats open instantly from a small saved copy (Settings > Advanced to clear it)
- Take a photo or record a video from the + button on phones
- Emoji and Save buttons when editing a message
- Performance report in Settings > Advanced
- Android installs updates itself (you confirm, and no APK is left behind)
- Desktop search box lists filters and recent searches when clicked, highlights matches and splits results into pages
- Click a video in a link preview to open it in the viewer and play it
- Formatting bar when editing a message
- Insert timestamp button (in the + menu on phones)
- Emoji pictures and favorite GIFs are saved on your device after the first time, so they show instantly, even offline

### Changed
- Big servers are much faster and no longer freeze
- Emoji show instantly, and your emoji style now applies everywhere
- Right-click menus reordered
- Videos and YouTube open in the media viewer, like pictures
- Dragging to rearrange is off on phones and tablets (Appearance > Rearrange by dragging)
- Clicking your own picture on your card opens your full profile (desktop)
- Android icon now matches Windows and Linux
- macOS 26 uses the new glass icon
- Screen share holds 30 fps (60 on the smooth option)
- Picture-in-picture and the mini call can be made smaller
- One search box on desktop, and clicking a result jumps to it in the chat
- Undeafening turns your mic back on
- Home, Favorites, folder and Add a server buttons get the panel blur
- Profiles and decorations show straight away on desktop (saved from last time)
- Emoji and GIFs share one picker with tabs; on phones it's a card from the bottom, and the emoji button turns into a keyboard while it's open
- Emoji hover cards and `:emoji` suggestions use your emoji style
- Discover opens inside Mustela on desktop instead of a separate window

### Fixed
- Channel list jumping when swiping back from a chat
- Hold menus closing as soon as you let go (voice channels included)
- Tablets: layout under the status bar, full-width user bar, search with the keyboard open, missing mini call button
- App staying zoomed in after leaving it or picture-in-picture on Android
- Settings headers overlapping while scrolling
- Profile cards: oversized picture, missing offline ring, wrong height
- Avatar decorations cut off in chat, member list and calls
- Spoilered links showing their preview, or a second uncovered one
- Spoilered video links not playing after being revealed
- Empty link cards and duplicate cards for one link (Instagram, Tumblr and others)
- Codes like `&#x2019;` in link titles
- Tray icon size
- Android insets after switching navigation mode
- DMs opening at an old message every time
- Search loading for a long time, and its text being smaller than the chat
- Updates offered for a release with no file for your system
- Formatting bar cut off at the edge of the message box
- Marking an attachment as a spoiler hiding its buttons
- Parts of the media viewer not closing it when clicked (desktop)
- Emoji picker's side buttons not always jumping to their section
