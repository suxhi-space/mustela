## Version: 0.1.8-build2 (full change list)

### New
- **Mustela servers** (renamed from "Profile servers" / "sync servers": they hold more than profiles). A Mustela server keeps what Stoat has no place for: decorations, frames, effects, nameplates, profile colours, per-server banners and what you're playing.
  - **Mustela's own server** (mustela.suxhi.space) is in everyone's list already. Press **Link** once to see other people's extras and share yours; nothing of yours is sent until you do.
  - Removing it keeps it removed on every device; the **Use** button in Settings > Sync & servers brings it back.
  - You can still add your own server by its address. A ready setup (Docker, HTTPS, daily backups) and a step-by-step guide are in `docs/hosting.md`.
  - Linking works as before: Mustela sends the server your Stoat sign-in once, so it can ask Stoat who you are. The server never stores or logs it.
  - **Private by design:** everything travels encrypted (HTTPS), what the server stores is encrypted on its disk, link keys are kept only as one-way hashes, nothing about your requests is logged, and "Who can see my profile" is enforced by the server itself. **Unlink** deletes everything the server keeps about you (profile, banners, activity, every device's link).
  - **Only for Mustela:** a server only answers people who linked it, so opening a profile address in a web browser shows nothing. What you're playing only goes to your friends and people you share a Stoat server with, even when your profile is for everyone.
  - "Keep a copy in my banner" stays on by default, for people who don't use a server.
- **Several activities at once**, like Discord. Every Rich Presence app and every detected game shows (a game *and* music), on your card, in Active now and for friends.
  - They keep a steady order (games first, then music), and on computers a profile card shows the first one as a stack; click it to see them all in the full profile. Voice lists show the app's own icon.
- **Your own games and apps** (Settings > Activity > Your games and apps > Add a program): pick a running program (Krita, an indie game), give it a name and choose Playing, Listening to or Watching. Mustela then detects it like a game.
- **Screen share sound on Mac and Linux.** The share picker offers the same choice as Windows: **This app** (the window you share), **Everything but Mustela**, or **No sound**. Mustela's own sound (the call) is always left out, so nobody hears themselves back.
  - Linux uses PipeWire: the app's sound (including its helper processes, like a browser's) is wired straight into the share, and new sounds (a new tab, the next song) are picked up as they start. Microphones and other devices are never included.
  - Mac uses Apple's ScreenCaptureKit (macOS 13 and newer).
  - If the sound can't start, the picture still goes out and a short message says why.
- **Other people's sounds** (Settings > Voice & Video): a volume just for you for everyone else's soundboard. While someone's sound plays, they're turned down to it (their voice too for those seconds, since the sound travels with their microphone). Works with every Mustela user, no Mustela server needed. "Your sounds" is the old soundboard volume.
- **View a server as a role** (Server settings > Roles, the eye button): see the channels and permissions as that role. A bar at the top stops it; changes are paused while you preview.
- **Windows on ARM** (Snapdragon laptops) and **Linux on ARM** (Raspberry Pi 5, Asahi Linux, ARM laptops). Updates pick the right file for your chip.
- **Invites with limits** (Stoat 0.16): server menu > Invite with limits, with "Expire after" (30 minutes to never) and "Max uses". The invite list shows how many times each was used and when it expires.
- **.rpm** for Fedora and openSUSE, next to the AppImage and .deb. Updates for it work like the .deb: Mustela downloads it and opens your system's installer.
- **Streams in PiP** (Android, Settings > Voice & Video): picture-in-picture while you're watching a stream, the call bubble the rest of the time.

### Improved
- **Activity reaches friends and your other devices.** The server's limits were shared between reading and writing, so a busy member list could use them up and "Playing…" was refused. They're separate and much higher now. A device with nothing playing no longer wipes what your computer sent, and a send that failed is tried again after 15 seconds.
- **Profiles on screen refresh every minute**, so activity shows without reopening the card.
- **Your own card** shows "In voice", and on your phone the activity your computer sends.
- **Rich Presence to Discord:** when Discord opens after a game already connected to Mustela, Mustela passes the game's activity on to Discord (and clears it when the game closes).
- **Phones: swipe the call down** to minimise it. It follows your finger and springs back on a short pull.
- **Easier to read colours.**
  - Name colours that almost disappear on the background (a very light pink on light mode, a very dark one on dark mode) are shown in a deeper shade of the same colour. The colour people picked never changes, only how it's shown. Role colours too.
  - No grey cushion behind names on nameplates, and glows always use the colour as picked.
  - Profile cards check names against the card's own colour; buttons ("Message", "Edit profile") use that person's colours instead of your theme; headings and the activity box are checked against the card's background.
  - The status box on your card uses the card's text colour.
  - Offline people fade less in light mode.
- **Updates on computers** don't show a card anymore (they install when Mustela starts). An update that's waiting (a .deb or .rpm, or one found with Check in Settings) shows as a small green download button in the title bar, or at the bottom of the server list with the system's title bar.
- **Phones:** the update card only has Update and Later.
- **Call messages** say how long the call lasted (Stoat 0.15's call event).
- **Deleting a server you own or a bot** asks for your password or 2FA code first, as Stoat 0.17 requires.
- **Emoji from other servers** are greyed out in the picker and for reactions in servers that don't allow them ("Use external emojis", Stoat 0.16), instead of failing to send.
- **Lighter traffic:** Mustela asks a Mustela server for many profiles in one request (server 1.4; older servers still work one by one).
- **Signed updates:** release files are signed with Mustela's own update key, and installed copies only install updates signed by it (on top of GitHub's checksum). Forks building from their own releases sign with their own key.
- **Tidier code:** about 120 unused style rules and leftover code removed.
- Stoat's JavaScript library updated to its newest version.

### Fixed
- **Mac:** messages, pictures and avatars drawn over the "You're viewing older messages" bar.
- **Mustela servers:** if a server forgets your device (for example its data was reset), Mustela links it again by itself, at start-up and when a change is refused. Only if that fails does it tell you, and Settings shows Link again.
- The updater offered .deb installs to .rpm and Flatpak installs.

### Good to know
- **Mac:** sharing sound needs macOS 13 or newer. It uses the Screen Recording permission you already gave Mustela (macOS calls it "Screen & System Audio Recording"). Older Macs still run Mustela, just without the sound.
- **Linux:** sharing sound needs PipeWire, the default on most distros now (Fedora, Ubuntu 22.10+, Mint 22, PikaOS and others). On Wayland, some windows don't say which app they belong to; for those, the picker only offers everything but Mustela.
- **Windows:** the call pop-out is a real Mustela window: Mustela's title bar (following the Title bar setting), "Mustela call" as its title, Mustela's icon in the taskbar, no address bar. If it ever freezes, close Mustela and open it again: it switches to Windows' own pop-up and tells you (Settings > Voice & Video > Call pop-out switches back). `popout.log` in Mustela's log folder shows where it stopped.
- **Sharing everything but Mustela** includes notification sounds and anything else playing on your computer.
- **Other people's sounds** only works between Mustela users; people on Stoat's own apps hear sounds as before.
- **Your own programs** and game detection read the list of running programs on your computer; nothing about them leaves your device except the name you gave, and only if you share your activity.
- **Running a Mustela server:** update it to 1.5 (`bash update.sh` in `server/deploy`; the first time, run it twice) for batched profiles, several activities, app icons, encrypted storage and deleting on unlink. Keep a copy of the `DATA_KEY` line from `.env` somewhere safe. Older servers still work, with one activity each.
